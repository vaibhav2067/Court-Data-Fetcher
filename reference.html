HIGH COURT OF DELHI

Civil Appeal No. CA/2024/1247

RAJESH KUMAR SHARMA

Appellant

Vs.

STATE OF DELHI & ANR.

Respondents

ORDER

This matter came up for hearing today. The learned counsel for the appellant submitted that the impugned order passed by the Additional District Judge is contrary to the established principles of law and the same deserves to be set aside.

After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and perusing the record, this Court is of the opinion that the matter requires detailed consideration.

List the matter for final hearing on 22nd August, 2024.

Justice A.K. Mendiratta
